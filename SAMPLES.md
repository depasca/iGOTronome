# Recorded one-shots

Files under `GOTronome/Samples/` replace the synthesized voice of the same name
at launch (see `SampleLoader.swift`). Mono 16-bit WAV, peak-normalised on load.
Kept byte-identical with the Android app's `app/src/main/assets/samples/`.
